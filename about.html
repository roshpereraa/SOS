Our story

Built in the edit bay, not the boardroom

SOS started the way most good studios do — one editor, a pair of headphones, and an obsession with why some videos get finished and others get skipped.

Work with us

Yuhan Perera founded SOS in Colombo with a simple working rule: the edit is downstream of the idea. Packaging, pacing and payoff get decided before a single clip is cut.

That rule is why a supplement-ranking video for Dr Karan cleared 10% CTR and a million views, and why a geopolitics series for Business Basics keeps crossing five million. Same method, different niche.

Early 2022

The ZMDE sprint

We uploaded 15 videos in 30 days with the ZMDE team, taking the channel past 10 million views a month.

3+ years

The Business Basics partnership

A relationship that began under 200k subscribers and now sits at 900k+, with several videos crossing 5 million views — including their most-viewed upload.

Today

164M+ views in 2026 alone

Across cybersecurity, geopolitics, medicine, motorsport and commerce — creators and brands of all sizes, in any niche.

How we think

Four things we don’t compromise on

Every project opens with an audit of your niche, audience and channel insights. The cut only works if the idea underneath it does.

Sharp cuts, rich colour grading, motion graphics and sound design are not decoration. Each one is tuned to keep a viewer watching to the end.

A creative alliance

Deadlines are a promise
